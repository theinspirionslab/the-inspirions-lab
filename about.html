The Foundation

Experience translated into a way of thinking.

The Inspirions Lab originates from a long-standing fascination with complex systems: how technology, people, processes and information interact to create results in practice.

A socio-technical foundation

My foundation was built in marine engineering, an environment where reliability depends on more than technology alone.

A ship is a socio-technical system: equipment, procedures, human decisions and operational reality must continuously work together.

From technology to systems thinking

Throughout my career, this perspective expanded beyond engineering into project leadership, business development, operational improvement and organisational transformation.

The recurring question became:

How can we design structures that enable people to perform, collaborate and learn effectively?

Transformation and entrepreneurship

As co-founder of Hoftel Föhr, I experienced the complete lifecycle of a complex transformation: from concept development and financing, to construction, operations and continuous improvement.

This reinforced the understanding that organisations are not collections of separate activities, but connected systems where people, processes, information and purpose influence each other.

The Inspirions Lab

Since 2020, The Inspirions Lab has been the platform where these experiences come together.

It explores how technology, learning and collaboration can reinforce each other through practical structures that reduce friction and enable meaningful progress.

Current focus

Today, the focus is on complex environments where technology, education and collaboration meet:

  • Interim Project Leadership & PMO+
  • Collaborative workspace design and knowledge structures
  • Technology education and talent ecosystems
  • AI-supported ways of working and systems thinking

The goal remains consistent: creating structures that give people the space to contribute, learn and create impact.
