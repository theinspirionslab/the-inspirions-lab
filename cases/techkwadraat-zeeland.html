PMO+ for Techkwadraat Zeeland
Structure that strengthens collaboration

A working exploration of programme support, professionalisation and regional impact.

Why this page exists

This page was created as a working exploration of the PMO+ role within Techkwadraat Zeeland.

Instead of presenting only experience and qualifications, it demonstrates how I analyse complex assignments, identify opportunities and translate ambitions into practical structures.

Reading the assignment

The role described is not only about planning, documentation and reporting.

The deeper challenge is creating an environment where regional coordinators, schools and partners can focus on their mission: inspiring young people towards technology.

The PMO+ perspective

A strong PMO does not create bureaucracy. It creates clarity.

  • One shared overview
  • Reliable information
  • Early identification of risks
  • Knowledge that remains available

First hypotheses

My first hypotheses would be:

  • Professionalisation should reduce workload, not increase it.
  • Reporting should create insight, not only accountability.
  • Knowledge management can strengthen regional cooperation.
