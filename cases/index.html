Cases & Experiments

Real situations. Open questions.

The cases in The Inspirions Lab come from different places: lived experience, projects, design work and questions we are still exploring.

They are not presented as proof of a fixed theory. Some are grounded in direct experience. Others are experiments, reconstructions or hypotheses. The distinction matters.

What connects them is a curiosity about the systems underneath: how people, information, technology, resources, purpose and structure influence what becomes possible.

Questions We're Exploring

Not every useful question has an answer yet.

Some of the work at The Inspirions Lab starts with an observation that does not yet have a satisfying explanation.

We formulate possible explanations, test them against experience, and actively look for counterexamples. A hypothesis is useful precisely because it can still be wrong.

What if we designed around potential rather than existing roles?

Organisations often begin with functions, roles and job descriptions. What happens when we begin instead with the capabilities, curiosity and potential already present in people?

HYPOTHESIS · OPEN

Why do we keep improving systems that may have been designed around the wrong assumption?

Improvement is useful. But sometimes the constraint is not inside the system. It is the way the system itself was framed.

QUESTION · EXPLORING

What becomes possible when information moves before decisions are made?

Many organisations invest heavily in tools while information remains scattered across people, departments and systems. Could changing the flow of information change the quality of decisions before adding more technology?
